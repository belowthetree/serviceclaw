## Integration Points

### Service Installation
When a Service installs with a cron trigger:
1. ServiceCronTrigger.create() validates the schedule
2. Creates CronJobCreate with service metadata
3. Calls cronService.add() to create the job
4. Stores trigger reference in ServiceRuntimeRefs

### Service Enable/Disable
When a Service is enabled/disabled:
1. Service.enable() calls cronTrigger.enable()
2. Service.disable() calls cronTrigger.disable()
3. Bulk operations disable/enable all jobs with matching serviceId

### Service Uninstall
When a Service is uninstalled:
1. Service.uninstall() calls cronTrigger.remove()
2. Bulk removes all jobs with matching serviceId

## Type Safety

- CronJob (stored) → listByService() → filtered results
